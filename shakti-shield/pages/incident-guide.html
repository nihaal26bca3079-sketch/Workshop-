        Understanding Zero FIR, Free Copy & Police Refusal

        Your basic rights when reporting a crime under BNSS (verify from official sources).

        Zero FIR & e-FIR (BNSS Section 173)

        A Zero FIR can be registered at any police station regardless of where the offence happened, and the police must give you a copy of the FIR free of cost.

        If Police Refuse to Register an FIR

        1. Write directly to the Superintendent of Police explaining the incident and the refusal.

        2. If action is still not taken, approach the Magistrate under BNSS Section 175(3).
