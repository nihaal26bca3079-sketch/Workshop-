Self-Defence Basics

Simple awareness and escape-first steps to create space and reach safety.

Core Rule: Escape and Call for Help First

Always prioritise getting away, making loud noise to draw attention, and calling 112 over physical confrontation. Use physical moves only to break free and run to safety.
