Know the Facts: Rights & Reporting

Verified legal information for India. General information only, not legal advice.

Current Criminal Laws (BNS, BNSS, BSA)

BNS replaced the IPC on 1 July 2024; BNSS replaced the CrPC and BSA replaced the Evidence Act. Offences against women and children are grouped in Chapter V of the BNS.

Zero FIR & Free Copy

Under BNSS Section 173, a Zero FIR can be registered at any police station regardless of where the offence happened, and the FIR copy is free.

If Police Refuse an FIR

Write to the Superintendent of Police, then approach the Magistrate under BNSS Section 175(3).

Workplace & Domestic Protection

Workplace sexual harassment is covered under POSH Act 2013 (complaints can also go through the SHe-Box portal; verify). Domestic violence is covered under Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005 (verify).
